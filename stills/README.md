# Key-art stills

Operator renders of the Kepler-62 Stapledon swarm. System scale. Not Habitat-kit proofs. Not Habitat Cuts.

| File | Camera | What it locks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`01-eclipse.jpg`](01-eclipse.jpg) | Wide, star-centered. Large irregular body in silhouette against Kepler-62. | Large body is non-spherical. Swarm is a veil of many cells, not a shell. Star is a K disk with prominences. |
| [`02-lattice.jpg`](02-lattice.jpg) | Wide, star in the upper right. Receding rows of cells. | Distant cells read as repeating sharp geometric silhouettes. Faint red orbital traces / haze allowed. |
| [`03-inward.jpg`](03-inward.jpg) | Inside the swarm, looking in. Foreground irregular body. Fine lattice over the disk. | Same large-body rule. A small circular transit disk may sit on the star. That disk is not the large body. |

## Rules

- The large irregular silhouette is **not** Kepler-62b–f. Do not label it. Do not name it.
- A small circular disk on the star may be a published planet. Do not assert which one. Do not treat it as the large body.
- These frames do not set cell count, plate thickness, or fill fraction. Those stay OPEN.
- Habitat-kit does not recreate these frames. It builds one close-up cell. The diamond / arrowhead marks in these stills are distant silhouettes, not the Habitat-kit mesh.
- Habitat Cuts (`construction` / `operational` / `relic`) are not in this folder. They are still unshot.
