# Key-art stills

Operator renders of the Kepler-62 Stapledon swarm. System scale. Not Habitat-kit proofs. Not Habitat Cuts.

![Kepler-62 eclipsed by the irregular key-art body; swarm as a cell veil](01-eclipse.jpg)

*`01-eclipse.jpg` — Wide, star-centered. Kepler-62 as an orange-yellow K disk with prominences. Large irregular, non-spherical body in silhouette. Not Kepler-62b–f. Swarm as a multi-layer veil of independent dark cells, not a shell. Locks: large-body shape class, K-disk, veil (not shell).*

![Receding lattice of dark geometric cells; K-disk in the upper right](02-lattice.jpg)

*`02-lattice.jpg` — Wide. Star in the upper right. Distant cells as repeating sharp geometric silhouettes (arrowhead / diamond / triangular). Rows recede. Faint red orbital traces and red-brown haze allowed. A small circular disk may sit on the limb — not the large body, planet ID OPEN. Locks: distant cell silhouette, traces / haze allowed.*

![Inward view: lattice over the disk, small circular transit, same irregular foreground body](03-inward.jpg)

*`03-inward.jpg` — Camera inside the swarm, looking in. Fine lattice over the disk. Same irregular foreground body as `01-eclipse`. Small circular transit disk is not the large body. Not a Habitat Cut. Locks: inward camera, lattice-over-disk, transit ≠ large body.*

## Rules

- The large irregular silhouette is **not** Kepler-62b–f. Do not label it. Do not name it.
- A small circular disk on the star may be a published planet. Do not assert which one. Do not treat it as the large body.
- These frames do not set cell count, plate thickness, or fill fraction. Those stay OPEN.
- Habitat-kit does not recreate these frames. It builds one close-up cell. The diamond / arrowhead marks in these stills are distant silhouettes, not the Habitat-kit mesh.
- Habitat Cuts (`construction` / `operational` / `relic`) are not in this folder. They are still unshot.
