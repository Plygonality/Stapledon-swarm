# Operator rules

Constraints for anyone who authors, revises, or loads this bible. Not world facts. World facts live in `bible/`.

## What this repo is for

Ship a versioned, loadable world frame for one independently orbiting cell in the Kepler-62 Stapledon swarm. Habitat-kit reads it. Time-slice maps three epochs onto it. Blend-ci cooks stills of it. This repo does none of that work.

## What this repo is not

- Not Habitat-kit. Do not add generators, graphs, or apply scripts.
- Not Blend-ci. Do not add cooks, hashes, or CI test suites.
- Not a swarm simulator.
- Not a story file to drop into [Hard-SciFi-idea-generator](https://github.com/Plygonality/Hard-SciFi-idea-generator).
- Not a second Unit-canon. Scale lives at https://github.com/Plygonality/Unit-canon
- Not a novel bible. No named characters, factions, religions, or generation-ship endings.

## Tone

Absolute directness. Numbers, failure modes, marked unknowns. No mythic diction. No “ancient builders,” no “fallen empire,” no “last ship.”

## Payload class (do not relax)

Transit hardware is gram-probes, dormant whole-brain emulations, and digital archives. Living crews on a 5 000 yr clock are out of scope. Do not add a generation ship to make the still warmer.

## Sol vs Kepler

Sol is the origin industrial base. Kepler-62 is the destination swarm. Do not light a Kepler cell with a G2 disk. Do not put Earth-flux panels on a 0.020 S☉ orbit and call it operational. Label the split on every still that could be misread.

## Speed

0.2c is a peak. Bang-coast-bang at ~0.001 g. Do not write “cruise at 0.2c for 982 yr.”

## Mass table

1 mm of plate covering 1% of a 3.6 AU sphere is ≈ 0.012 M⊕. 12 M⊕ is a 1 m plate at 1%. The 12 M⊕ figure is a thickness error, not a mass budget. Do not restore it.

## Key-art body

The large body in the key-art still is not Kepler-62b, c, d, e, or f. Do not retcon the silhouette onto those planets. Do not name the body to close the question.

## OPEN items

Do not fill them in a still brief, a graph comment, or a README flourish. Promote them only with [`../prompts/revise-bible.md`](../prompts/revise-bible.md) and a CHANGELOG entry.

## Artwork

Describe stills in text. Do not commit image binaries to this repo.

## Tags

Preserve every CANON / INFERENCE / OPEN tag. Definitions: [`../schema/status-tags.md`](../schema/status-tags.md).
