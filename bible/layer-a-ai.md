## Swarm

Independently orbiting cells. Outer bound 3.6 AU. Kepler-62b–f sit inside ~0.72 AU. Those five are not the swarm.

Mass table (correction): 1 mm of plate on 1% of a 3.6 AU sphere ≈ 0.012 M⊕. 12 M⊕ buys a 1 m plate at the same fill. The old 12 M⊕ @ 1 mm number is a unit slip. Delete it.

Foil at 1 mm is cheap in planetary mass. A 1 m plate at 1% is a small planet taken apart. Cell count, fill, and alloy stay OPEN.
