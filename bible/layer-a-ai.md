## Frame

Destination is Kepler-62, 982 ly from Sol, work figures 0.76 M☉ and 0.26 L☉. Swarm outer radius is 3.6 AU. Flux there is ≈ 0.020 S☉. Equilibrium-temperature work figure is ~120 K. Origin industrial base is Sol. Do not light a Kepler cell with a G2 disk. Do not mix Sol hardware into a Kepler still without labeling the split.

Peak speed is 0.2c. That is a peak, not a cruise average. The profile is bang-coast-bang at ~0.001 g. Transit is ~5000–5100 yr. Light-travel is 982 yr; a radio reply is ~1964 yr. Do not stage a conversation.

Payload class on the hop: gram-probes, dormant whole-brain emulations, digital archives. No generation ship. No named crew. No factions. No religions. No endings.

## Swarm

Independently orbiting cells, not a rigid shell, not a ringworld. Outer bound 3.6 AU. The five published planets (Kepler-62b–f) orbit inside ~0.72 AU. They are not the swarm.

Mass table (correction): 1 mm of plate covering 1% of a 3.6 AU sphere is ≈ 0.012 M⊕. 12 M⊕ is a 1 m plate at the same fill. The old 12 M⊕ @ 1 mm figure is a millimeter/meter slip. Delete it.

A thin-foil swarm is cheap in planetary mass. A meter-class plate swarm is a disassembled small planet. Cell count, fill fraction, and alloy are OPEN.

## Cell

Habitat-kit may build **one cell**. Scale lives in Unit-canon (https://github.com/Plygonality/Unit-canon): 1.0 m grid, 3.0 m deck, 1.0 m airlock, 1.80 m human figure. Do not copy those numbers into graphs as if this repo owns them.

Habitat Cuts are Time-slice epochs on the same hull: `construction` / `operational` / `relic`. Epoch is a parameter. It does not redesign the cell.

Exterior is IR-dark. Visible key is work lights, not the star. The star is a K2 disk at 0.26 L☉, not Sol. Shirtsleeve balconies at 120 K are a fail.

Atmosphere mix, power architecture, and whether planets e/f are feedstock are OPEN.
